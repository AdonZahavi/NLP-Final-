# Segmented evaluation subsets (issue #6)

Generated by `segmentation/segment_subsets.py` running **YAP** (chosen in
issue #5, see `segmentation/README.md`) over the fixed subsets in
`data/subsets/`. Files mirror the raw subsets 1:1 by `id`; each text field
gains a `<field>_seg` twin (sentiment: `text_seg`; NLI: `premise_seg`,
`hypothesis_seg`; QA: `context_seg`, `question_seg`).

## Surface format decision

Morphemes are joined by **single spaces**, within and across tokens:

> raw: וכשהלכתי הביתה — seg: ו כש הלכתי ה ביתה

Rationale: this is the convention of the gold `morph` variant of the omilab
sentiment benchmark and of morpheme-level Hebrew NLP work (Amram et al. 2018),
and it needs no special separator explanation in LLM prompts.

## Properties to remember when interpreting results

1. **YAP output is a morphological analysis, not a pure surface split.** It
   reconstructs the covert definite article (במקום → ב ה מקום) and maps
   pronominal suffixes to base pronouns (בך → ב אתה). The segmented condition
   therefore contains morphemes absent from the raw surface text.
2. Long QA contexts were sentence-split before segmentation and rejoined.
3. Chunks where YAP failed fall back to raw surface tokens (count reported in
   the run log; expected ≈0).
4. Validation: `segmentation/gold_check.py` scores the segmented sentiment
   subset against the expert gold morphemes (`data/raw/morph_test.tsv`).
   `qc_sample.md` holds 30 random raw/seg pairs per task for manual review.
